## LIMITATIONS & ASSUMPTIONS

### Core function of this subsection
- Make the central assumptions of the design explicit.
- Show that you understand the weaknesses of the approach.
- Turn limitations into disciplined methodological transparency rather than into damage control.

### Subsection red thread
- Every design choice creates trade-offs.
- The corridor design improves flood relevance but narrows the population of inference.
- Municipality-level comparison is policy-relevant but ecologically coarse.
- Vulnerability indexing is useful but uncertain and sensitive to design choices.
- Protection remains conceptually central but empirically difficult to operationalize cleanly.

### Suggested paragraph structure

#### Paragraph 1 - Scope, corridor definition, and exposure metric
**Goal**
- Explain the first layer of assumptions: what exactly is being represented by the exposure side.

**Core claims to make**
- The corridor design includes only municipalities intersecting the `RP500` flood-relevant area.
- This sharpens the analysis but means that findings are specific to the flood-relevant Elbe corridor rather than to every municipality in the basin.
- The main exposure indicators are municipality-level flooded area shares.
- These indicators capture territorial exposure, not directly exposed persons, buildings, or losses.
