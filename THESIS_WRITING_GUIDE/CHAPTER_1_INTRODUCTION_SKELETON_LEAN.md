# 1. Introduction

## Overall job of Chapter 1
- open the topic
- establish relevance
- state the gap
- state the thesis contribution
- present the research questions
- orient the reader for the rest of the thesis

## One-sentence chapter logic
- Flood risk is becoming more socially and politically consequential under climate change, existing research still leaves a precise municipality-level distributive gap in the Elbe corridor, and this thesis addresses that gap through a justice-oriented analysis of flood exposure, vulnerability, and later protection-related differentiation.

## Things Chapter 1 must not do
- do full justice theory
- do full socio-hydrology theory
- define the entire conceptual triad in detail
- explain the corridor and municipality logic at method depth
- become a miniature version of Chapters 2 and 3

## Recommended chapter rhythm
- `MOTIVATION & PROBLEM FRAMING`: 4 to 5 paragraphs
- `RESEARCH GAP & THESIS CONTRIBUTION`: 2 to 3 paragraphs
- `RESEARCH QUESTIONS & ANALYTICAL ORIENTATION`: 1 to 2 paragraphs
- `THESIS OUTLINE`: 1 short paragraph
