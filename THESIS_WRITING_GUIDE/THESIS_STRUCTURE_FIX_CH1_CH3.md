# Thesis Structure Fix for Chapters 1-3

Date: 2026-03-25

This note fixes the current overlap between `Chapter 1`, `Chapter 2`, and `Chapter 3`.

The thesis does **not** need a full redesign.
The title stays unchanged.
The two research questions stay unchanged.

What needs to change is the **division of labor** between the early chapters.

---

## Core diagnosis

The current versions of Chapters 1-3 repeat the same core ideas too often:
- justice matters
- flood exposure is socially uneven
- the Elbe is a strong case
- municipalities are the right scale
- exposure, vulnerability, and protection belong together

None of these points are wrong.
The problem is that they currently appear in too many places with too similar a function.

That weakens the red thread.

---

## New governing rule

- `Chapter 1 = why this thesis`
- `Chapter 2 = how this thesis thinks`
- `Chapter 3 = how this thesis works`

This is now the clean master logic for the whole front half of the thesis.

---

## Revised chapter responsibilities

## Chapter 1

### Job
- motivate the topic
- open the distributive puzzle
- define the gap
- state the thesis contribution
- present the research questions
- orient the reader

### Chapter 1 must do
- show that floods matter materially, politically, and socially
- make clear that adaptation and protection are not socially neutral
- define the gap modestly and precisely
- state what this thesis contributes
- present the fixed research questions
- close with a short thesis outline

### Chapter 1 must not do
- build the full justice theory
- define procedural / distributive / recognition justice in depth
- explain socio-hydrology in detail
- fully explain the exposure-vulnerability-protection triad
- justify the municipality scale at full length
- present the full research design logic

### Revised Chapter 1 structure
1. `Motivation & Problem Framing`
2. `Research Gap & Thesis Contribution`
3. `Research Questions & Analytical Orientation`
4. `Thesis Outline`

### Important structural fix
- `Climate Change, Floods & Justice` should **not** remain a large standalone subsection.
- Its strongest material should be redistributed:
  - part into the end of `Motivation & Problem Framing`
  - part into the transition toward `Research Gap & Thesis Contribution`

Reason:
- the justice lens must appear early
- but the full conceptual work belongs in Chapter 2

---

## Chapter 2

### Job
- provide the conceptual and theoretical backbone
- define the key terms and debates
- situate the German and Elbe case
- explain why flood risk is a social and political problem, not only a physical one

### Chapter 2 keeps its core structure
1. `Flood Risk Management in Germany`
2. `Area of Interest`
3. `Environmental & Flood Justice`
4. `Socio-Hydrology and Human Dimension of Risk`
5. `Conceptual Framing of This Study`

### Chapter 2 must do
- carry the heavy conceptual work
- define the theoretical language of the thesis
- give the reader the interpretive framework for later chapters

### Chapter 2 must not do
- resell the thesis contribution
- restate the gap at length
- explain the exact corridor design in operational detail
- become a methods chapter

---

## Chapter 3

### Job
- translate the Chapter-2 framework into the actual study design
- explain what is being compared, at what scale, and with what analytical logic
- define the design boundaries of the thesis

### Chapter 3 must do
- describe the empirical study design
- define the study area and unit of analysis
- explain the equity-assessment logic
- state scope conditions, assumptions, and limits

### Chapter 3 must not do
- restate the research questions as a full subsection
- rebuild the conceptual model from scratch
- repeat the justice theory from Chapter 2
- do detailed data-processing work that belongs in Chapter 4

### Revised Chapter 3 structure
1. `Overall Approach`
2. `Study Area and Spatial Unit of Analysis`
3. `Equity Assessment Logic`
4. `Scope, Assumptions & Limits`

### Important structural fix
- `Research Questions` should be removed as a standalone Chapter 3 subsection.
Reason:
  - they belong in Chapter 1.

- `Conceptual Model` should also be removed as a standalone Chapter 3 subsection.
Reason:
  - the conceptual work belongs in Chapter 2.
  - if needed, the model can appear as:
    - one figure at the end of Chapter 2, or
    - one short bridging paragraph at the start of Chapter 3.

## Revised red thread across chapters

### Chapter 1
Floods matter, their impacts are socially differentiated, and this thesis addresses a specific gap by examining distributive flood patterns in the Elbe corridor.

### Chapter 2
Flood risk, vulnerability, protection, and justice need to be understood conceptually before they can be analyzed empirically.

### Chapter 3
These concepts are translated into a municipality-based corridor design that compares socially differentiated exposure patterns within clearly defined analytical limits.

---

## Working recommendation from now on

From this point forward, write with the following priority:

1. `Chapter 2` remains the main theory container.
2. `Chapter 1` must stay lean and strategic.
3. `Chapter 3` must stay lean and design-focused.

Whenever a paragraph starts doing too many of these things at once:
- motivating
- theorizing
- defining concepts
- selling the contribution
- explaining the method

split it and move it to the right chapter.
